# Reading room redesign

Seed key: fc25957e. The owner delegated visual decisions; no approval round is required.
Mode: Operate for the shell/library; Read for the reader.

## DESIGN THESIS
A personal reading room: a persistent forest-ink directory beside a generous paper reading desk, with typography and book covers carrying the identity.

## WORLD
Grounded candidates: independent bookshop tables; annotated literary journals; library catalogue drawers; e-reader collections; reading room directories (assigned fifth); audiobook listening desks; serial-fiction periodicals. The assigned fifth combines a fixed directory with a calm working desk.
The sneaker archive challenger is competitive for collection browsing; retain disciplined cover alignment. Cloud diffraction is declined: retain quiet margins. Aircraft instruments are declined: retain explicit operational status. ASCII scenes are declined: retain concise information density. Signal-grid identity is declined: retain consistent active-state contrast. Star atlases are competitive for Codex exploration: retain a stable chapter boundary. Neither competitive challenger improves both reading clarity and audience identification enough to replace the assigned reading room.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
A forest directory identifies the product and major destinations. The main reading desk opens with a large literary greeting, then a titled continue-reading feature with a true resume chapter, book cover and reading action. Secondary stories and background jobs have a quieter column; no invented stats or marketing claims.

## VISITOR PATH
Return to the current chapter from Home; browse and filter the Library; import new books; inspect background work; use the spoiler-bounded Codex from a novel. Mobile keeps search reachable and places primary navigation at the bottom.

## SIGNATURE INTERACTION
The reading bookmark: a precise slim progress line travels through the chosen accent as the reader advances. Cover actions appear on hover and keyboard focus and remain visible on touch. Reduced motion preserves every action.

## SYSTEM AND RISK
Use existing self-hosted Newsreader/Hanken typography; increase hierarchy, simplify chrome, use flat book-jacket placeholders and restrained rounded controls. Preserve saved appearance choices. Dark theme remains fully functional. A quiet design risks underselling operational capability: keep job state and import actions visible. Implementation is code-led under the owner's delegated control, recorded for this task only rather than as a standing preference.
